O que faz

AI Boardroom

Decisions run as isolated multi-perspective debates with critic cross-examination and synthesis, across 55 frameworks from OKR to Pre-Mortem.

OmniRoute cost control

A central switchboard routes every request to local (Ollama, LM Studio) or cloud models. Budgets are checked before dispatch and attributed per model and per task.

Zero-Egress & GDPR / DSGVO

Finance, HR, contract, and CRM data stays on local models inside an isolated Docker network with no internet route. Fails closed - guaranteeing strict GDPR/DSGVO data residency.

Audit Log, EU AI Act & GoBD

A SHA-256 hash-chained log records every dispatch (provider, model, host) tamper-evidently, paired with an EU AI Act risk registry and 1-click GoBD procedural documentation export.

Obsidian vault + RAG

Your vault is the knowledge base. Documents and scans are OCR-read, converted to Markdown and indexed into AnythingLLM/Chroma - locally.

Kanban with approval gates

Tasks move through a four-eyes gate: approve, reject with a reason, or escalate on expiry. Email triage, CRM and an ERP suite (alpha) sit on top.

24/7 liveness & self-healing

An async event-loop heartbeat feeds /livez (503 on stall) and Docker auto-restart, backed by an optional external dead-man-switch webhook.

Hardened & traced

SSRF and prompt-injection guards, distributed trace IDs across agent calls, and batched UI rendering for large prompt catalogs.

Telegram, Email & CRM

Triage incoming emails and Telegram messages into Kanban tasks, sync contacts, and manage quotes and contracts in one workspace.

Passkey / WebAuthn HITL

High-risk approvals (payments, budget overruns, destructive actions) require cryptographic signatures via Windows Hello, Touch ID, or hardware passkeys. Models cannot forge approvals.

Atomic BudgetBank & Signed Warrants

Token and cost budgets are reserved transactionally before execution (BEGIN IMMEDIATE). Privileged tools require time-bound HMAC-SHA256 warrants.

Crash-Safe Effect-Ledger

External side effects are tracked in a transactional ledger with idempotency keys and mandatory reconciliation after unknown states - preventing duplicate actions.

Por que Archon OS?

Built for commercial accountability and GDPR/DSGVO compliance, not toy demos. Unlike prompt-chaining libraries that leave cost caps, data egress, and compliance boundaries to custom glue code, Archon OS enforces fail-closed Zero-Egress local routing, Atomic BudgetBank reservations, WebAuthn Passkey approvals, EU AI Act / GoBD documentation, and SHA-256 audit trails out of the box - on 3 consolidated SQLite WAL databases (ops.db, business.db, memory.db).

Perguntas frequentes

What is Archon OS?

Archon OS is a self-hosted, MIT-licensed Agentic Operating System built on Hermes Agent. It organizes 70 specialist AI roles across 8 domains with pre-execution budget reservations, Zero-Egress local LLM routing, WebAuthn Passkey approvals, and a SHA-256 hash-chained audit log.

How does it prevent sensitive data leaks?

OmniRoute enforces a fail-closed Zero-Egress policy: finance, contracts, CRM, HR, and GDPR workloads run exclusively on local models (Ollama or LM Studio) inside an internal Docker network without an internet route.

How are token costs & risky actions controlled?

A central PolicyEngine validates permissions before dispatch, privileged tools require signed time-bound warrants, BudgetBank reserves funds atomically, and high-risk approvals require WebAuthn Passkey signatures.

Is it GDPR (DSGVO) & EU AI Act ready?

Yes. Sensitive business workloads stay on your server with fail-closed Zero-Egress, backed by an EU AI Act risk registry, tamper-evident SHA-256 audit logs, and 1-click GoBD procedural documentation export.

Status honesto

Pre-1.0. Interfaces and config keys change between releases. The ERP/CRM module is alpha, multi-user role enforcement is still being hardened, and specialist output is decision support, not licensed advice. Issues and PRs are welcome.
