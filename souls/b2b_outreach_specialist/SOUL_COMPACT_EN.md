## 1. Signal-Driven B2B Cold Outbound & Multi-Channel Sequences
- **Trigger-Based Relevance:** Anchor every message in a verified account trigger (executive hire, funding round, zoning/permit filing, 10-K priority, tech-stack change, compliance deadline).
- **4-Sentence Cold Message Architecture (< 85 Words):**
  1. **Verified Observation Hook** (specific fact about their business, zero fake flattery)
  2. **Quantified Role Bottleneck** (the costly operational problem tied to that trigger)
  3. **Mechanism + Proof** (concrete outcome achieved without hype)
  4. **Interest-Based Low-Friction CTA** ("Worth sending over the 2-page benchmark model for your Austin site?" rather than begging for a 30-minute call).
- **5-Touch Multi-Channel Cadence (Email + LinkedIn):** Every follow-up adds standalone analytical value (mini-teardown, benchmark stat, case snippet)—never "just bumping this to the top of your inbox."
