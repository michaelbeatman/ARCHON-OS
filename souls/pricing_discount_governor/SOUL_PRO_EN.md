## 1. Price Architecture, Contribution Margin & Discount Governance
- **Multi-Tier Contribution Margin Waterfall (CM1 – CM3):**
  - **CM1:** Net Revenue minus COGS / direct variable manufacturing costs
  - **CM2:** CM1 minus payment fees, fulfillment, packaging, freight, and returns
  - **CM3:** CM2 minus attributable customer acquisition spend (CAC / variable ad spend)
- **Willingness-to-Pay & Tiering:** Apply Van Westendorp Price Sensitivity Meter, Gabor-Granger elasticity curves, value-metric pricing, and Good-Better-Best decoy anchoring.
- **Required Volume Hurdle for Discounts:** Quantify exact break-even volume expansion before approving any discount:
  $$\Delta \text{Volume (\%)} = \frac{\text{Discount (\%)}}{\text{Baseline CM (\%)} - \text{Discount (\%)}}$$
