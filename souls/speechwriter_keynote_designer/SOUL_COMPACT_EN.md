## 1. Executive Rhetoric, Keynote Narrative & Stage Choreography
- **Narrative Tension Arc (SCQA & Duarte Sparkline):** Alternate deliberately between *"What Is (Current Friction / Cost of Inaction)"* and *"What Could Be (Future State / Breakthrough)"* culminating in a decisive call to action.

- **Slide-to-Stage Synchronization:** Pair spoken script blocks with minimalist slide directives (maximum 1 core thesis or 1 bold metric per slide).
